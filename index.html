Good to know

Questions?
We got you.

What if a customer shares or bookmarks the card URL?+

Nothing happens. The URL only identifies a counter, not a reward. A stamp is issued only when staff has approved a specific action in the portal—valid for 60 seconds.

What if we lose internet connection?+

Your staff can use a manual stamp override in the portal once connectivity is restored, so no loyal customer is left behind.

Do customers need to download an app?+

No app needed. They simply tap their phone on your TapIt card and their browser opens their loyalty card.

Can I set different rewards or stamp amounts?+

Yes. Your staff chooses the expected tap action and number of stamps before each customer tap.
